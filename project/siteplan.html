Site Plan: KNCC

Site Name: KNCC.SHOP

This name represent this handicraft shop was built by these two communities call the Nukuoro and Kapingamarangi to focus on helping the people of the community to display and sell their artworks.

Logo

Site Purpose

This website serves as link between community and outsite world. This connection will help the KNCC reaches its customers from around the world. By having this connection, KNCC will be able to display its purpose to the world.
